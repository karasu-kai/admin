04 · Why this

Slight rebrand, not a rebuild.

  • Fills the slot Live site already has .l-crow-slot waiting — this drops in without layout change.
  • Name-true Karasu = crow. Yatagarasu’s third leg makes it yours, not a stock raven.
  • On-system Same black field, #c8102e eye, geometric cut — no new palette or font.
  • Meaning Guide through complexity. Matches “brief to deployment, no drag.”
